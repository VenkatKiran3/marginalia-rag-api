# Velora Drive Firmware — Release Notes

Firmware updates are installed through the Velora app over Bluetooth. Keep the bike above 30% charge during an update.

## Version 3.4 (August 2026)

- **Smart Charge limit**: you can now cap charging at 80% to extend battery lifespan. Velora recommends enabling it if you mostly ride short distances.
- Walk Assist now holds 6 km/h on slopes up to 12%.
- Fixed: the display sometimes showed the wrong remaining range after a cold start below 5 °C.

## Version 3.3 (May 2026)

- **Range Assist**: the app estimates remaining range from your recent riding style, the selected assist mode and the elevation of the planned route.
- The battery-saver mode now activates automatically at 15% charge (previously 10%). In battery-saver mode, assistance is limited to Eco.
- Known issue: Range Assist estimates can be up to 20% too optimistic in temperatures below 0 °C. A fix is planned for version 3.5.

## Version 3.2 (February 2026)

- Added Turbo mode on the Aero X2, delivering the motor's full 80 Nm of torque.
- Automatic light sensor: the front and rear lights switch on when ambient light drops.
- Fixed: rare Bluetooth disconnects during firmware downloads on Android 15.
