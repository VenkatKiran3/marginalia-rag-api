# Velora Cycles Employee Handbook

Welcome to Velora. This handbook covers how we work together. It applies to all employees on a Dutch employment contract. Questions go to people@velora.example.

## Working hours and location

Our headquarters and workshop are in Utrecht, the Netherlands. Office staff work a hybrid schedule: at least two days per week in the Utrecht office, with Tuesday as the shared team day when everyone is expected on site. Workshop and assembly staff work fully on site.

Core collaboration hours are 10:00–15:00 CET. Outside those hours you can arrange your own schedule with your manager.

## Time off

### Vacation

Full-time employees receive 25 vacation days per year, plus Dutch public holidays. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next calendar year; carried-over days expire on 1 July.

### Parental leave

Velora tops up statutory parental leave so that the birth or adoptive parent receives 16 weeks of fully paid leave, and the partner receives 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave can be taken within the first year after the birth or adoption.

### Sick leave

Report sickness to your manager before 09:30 on the first day. You do not need a doctor's note for absences shorter than three days.

## Learning and development

Every employee has a personal learning budget of EUR 1,200 per calendar year for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over. Requests above EUR 500 for a single item need written approval from your manager before you book.

## Expenses

Submit expenses in the finance portal within 30 days of the purchase, with a photo of the receipt. Any single expense above EUR 500 needs manager approval before purchase. Travel between home and the office is reimbursed at EUR 0.23 per kilometre, or you can lease a Velora e-bike through the company bike plan.

## Company bike plan

Employees can lease any current Velora model through the bike plan. The lease runs for 36 months, payments are deducted from gross salary, and at the end of the lease you can buy the bike for 15% of its original retail price.
